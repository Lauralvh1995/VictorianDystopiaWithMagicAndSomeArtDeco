Mortal that ascended to [The High Heavens](../../Realms/The%20High%20Heavens.md) through their own power and sacrifice.

## Birth and Early Life
Ylothi was born a farmer's daughter 1453 years ago in a region of  [Wamthen](../../Countries/Wamthen/Wamthen.md), which would eventually become the [Cyrdanian Republic](../../Countries/Cyrdanian%20Republic/Cyrdanian%20Republic.md). She was the seventh child born in the family.
Her mother babied her, much to her father's dismay. Eventually, she found her role in the family as the person who would try to cheer everyone up when they're down. A good listener.

## Marriage
At age 21 she was to be married off to [Margeleth the Great](../Saints/Margeleth%20the%20Great.md) after he was forced to rest at the farm during the [Great War](The%20Great%20Wamthenian%20War). Margeleth took a liking to her presence, when she would sit with him in the middle of the night. He took her hand and poured out everything, in a manner never seen before. He found renewed strength and eventually won the war in Wamthen's favour. Rumors spread however, that Margeleth had ruined this younger woman.

After the war, Margeleth returned to the farm, looking for the younger woman that gave him strength. She was home, but maimed, beaten, and kept in a corner. No man would ever dare to touch her, having been touched by the Emperor, then discarded. Much to her father's surprise, he asked her father for her hand. He told the story of what happened that night, three years ago. She was not ruined. He would have the best surgeons in Wamthen look after her to restore her face and body. She was soon taken to the capital to be healed.
Margeleth and Ylothi married. The entire court was surprised, why would the Emperor marry a common woman?

## Court Life
Life at the Wamthenian court was turbulent. First, Ylothi had to adjust to what was expected of a noble. 

## Dreams of a Falling Sky
Ylothi fell into a weekslong coma when [[]] poisoned her. During her coma she witnessed a recurring vision of the sky falling down. Then, one iteration, she asked the sky why it fell. To her suprise [ the Sky](The%20Father%20Above%20) answered. He was lonely. He missed [ his wife](The%20Mother%20Below%20). He failed his children. He wanted to go to Her. 

And Ylothi raised up from her body, and flew up. In the heavens, she sat down with an elderly man. Clearly defeated. And she listened. Just as she had done with the Emperor. In the end, she embraced him. "I cannot replace her, but I can support you to the best of my ability. I will listen to your woes. And I do not expect anything in return" she said. The Great Father cried. And the sky stopped falling.

Looking down, Ylothi saw her husband distraught. The love of his life had still not woken up. Councilors were pushing for funeral arrangements to be made. To prepare to become a widower. 
Ylothi looked at the Father. "But right now, I am needed down there. When my time comes, may I listen to you again?"
The Father gave her his blessing, and sent her back down.

And Ylothi descended. And awoke. Margeleth cried tears of joy. She was back at his side. The two spoke of the visions. The changes that were happening in the realm.

In one meeting with the Senate, Ylothi spoke up. She spoke of  the falling skies. And that only when humans offered compassion to one another with no expectation of reward, the worst could be staved off. Her words fell on deaf ears. Senator [[]] even told her it was not her place to speak, and asked Emperor Margeleth to punish her for this transgression. With great displeasure, he gave in.

Eventually, Ylothi shared her mesage of compassion with the world outside the courts. There she gained a great following, and the message begun to spread.
