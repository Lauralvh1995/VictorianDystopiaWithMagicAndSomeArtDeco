Or Dream-Saint Alonith to some members of the [Church](The%20Holy%20Ylothian%20Church).
He became an [Everdreamer](Everdreamers) to be able to stay in [The Land of Dreams](../../Realms/The%20Land%20of%20Dreams.md) to try and convert the Fae themselves. 

His soul has withered over 300 years ago. When the Church moved to burn his body and shatter his statue, a sect shattered it and hid the pieces all over [The City of Cyrdan](../../Countries/Cyrdanian%20Republic/The%20City%20of%20Cyrdan/The%20City%20of%20Cyrdan.md).
